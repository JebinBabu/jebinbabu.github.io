About

I'm Jebin, an aspiring Computational Biologist and Bioinformatician. I sit at the intersection of biology, computation, and data — asking questions that neither field could answer alone. My work spans genomic data analysis, and bioinformatics pipeline development, with a focus on turning raw biological data into reproducible, scalable insight.
